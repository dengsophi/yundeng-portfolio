About

Yun Deng

London-based multidisciplinary designer working across lighting, scenography, live performance and interactive installation.

With a background in Theatre Design at the University of the Arts London and postgraduate studies in Light in Performance at Rose Bruford College, her practice brings together artistic enquiry, spatial composition and technical experimentation. Her professional experience spans theatre production in both London and Beijing, including a placement within the Lighting Department at the National Theatre’s Dorfman Theatre.

At the centre of her practice is an interest in light not simply as a means of illumination, but as a material capable of shaping space, perception, emotion and human behaviour.

Drawing on the relationship between the physical and the perceptual, her work often explores the boundaries between familiarity and estrangement, visibility and invisibility, reality and its mediated representations. Through light, sound, space and emerging technologies, she investigates how environments influence the ways in which we perceive, experience and respond to the world around us.

Her approach moves between conceptual thinking and practical realisation, treating design as both a creative discipline and a means of enquiry.
